Where the evidence points, by dimension

Civil liberties: Liberty-first (high confidence)
Years documenting police killings and leading protests. Detained in Tanzania in May 2025, where Amnesty International reports he was tortured. Charged in Kenya in July 2025 with possessing a blank round after terrorism accusations were dropped. He pleaded not guilty.
Political style: Anti-establishment (medium confidence)
An activist outside party politics who says he will campaign door to door and refuse alliances with ethnic power brokers.
Land, wealth and taxes: Strong redistribution (medium confidence, stated position)
S Campaigns on free education, health care and water, plus recovery of stolen public money.
Role of the state in the economy: Leans state-led (low confidence, stated position)
S Free public services imply a bigger state role. He has not set out a policy on ownership or markets.

What they appear to have stood for

A liberty-first, anti-establishment activist with a strongly redistributive programme. His economic plans are words only, because he has not held office.

How to read this page

Actions come first: laws, votes, decisions, court cases. Words count less, and only where the record is thin and not contradicted. A leader with real power now is judged on actions only.
