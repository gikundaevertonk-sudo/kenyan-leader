Government, opposition and the 2027 race

  • William RutoPresident since 2022. Deputy President 2013 to 2022. Party: UDA.
  • Kithure KindikiDeputy President since 1 November 2024. Interior CS 2022 to 2024. Tharaka-Nithi Senator 2013 to 2022. UDA.
  • Rigathi GachaguaDeputy President 2022 to Oct 2024 (impeached). Former Mathira MP. Leader of DCP.
  • Kalonzo MusyokaVice President 2008 to 2013. Former minister. Wiper leader. United Opposition principal.
  • Fred Matiang'iEducation CS 2015 to 2018. Interior CS 2018 to 2022. Jubilee leader.
  • Martha KaruaJustice Minister 2005 to 2009 (and earlier Water Minister). Former Gichugu MP. Leader of PLP.
  • David MaragaChief Justice 2016 to 2021. United Green Movement (UGM) presidential candidate for 2027.
  • Okiya OmtatahBusia Senator since 2022. Public-interest litigant. Leader of the National Reconstruction Alliance. Weighing a 2027 presidential run.
  • Boniface MwangiPhotojournalist and human rights activist. 2027 presidential aspirant.
  • Edwin SifunaNairobi Senator since 2017. Removed as ODM Secretary-General. Linda Mwananchi presidential candidate.
  • Ndindi NyoroKiharu MP. Former Budget Committee chair. Leader of the People's Party since Aug 2026.
  • Babu OwinoEmbakasi East MP since 2017. Nairobi governor aspirant.
  • Peter SalasyaMumias East MP since 2022. 2027 presidential aspirant.
  • Gladys WangaHoma Bay Governor since 2022. Part of the ratified ODM leadership team.
  • Millie OdhiamboSuba North MP. National Assembly Minority Chief Whip. ODM.
  • Karen NyamuNominated Senator (UDA) since 2022.
  • Millicent OmangaFormer nominated senator. Joined DCP in March 2026. Nairobi Woman Rep aspirant.
  • Anne WaiguruKirinyaga Governor since 2017. Former Devolution CS.

Earlier leaders

  • Jomo KenyattaPrime Minister 1963. President 1964 to 1978. KANU. Died 1978.
  • Daniel arap MoiVice President 1967 to 1978. President 1978 to 2002. KANU. Died 2020.
  • Mwai KibakiFinance Minister 1969 to 1982. Vice President 1978 to 1988. President 2002 to 2013. Died 2022.
  • Uhuru KenyattaDeputy Prime Minister 2008 to 2013. President 2013 to 2022. Jubilee. Retired; his record in office is complete.
  • Raila OdingaEnergy Minister 2001 to 2002. Roads Minister 2003 to 2005. Prime Minister 2008 to 2013. ODM. Died October 2025.
  • Jaramogi Oginga OdingaVice President 1964 to 1966. Founded KPU and later co-founded FORD. Died 1994.
  • Tom MboyaTrade unionist. Justice Minister 1963 to 1964. Economic Planning Minister 1964 to 1969. KANU Secretary-General. Assassinated 1969.
  • George SaitotiFinance Minister 1983 to 1993. Vice President 1989 to 1997 and 1999 to 2002. Internal Security Minister 2008 to 2012. Died 2012.
  • Ronald NgalaKADU leader 1960 to 1964. Leader of Government Business before independence. Later KANU minister. Died 1972.
  • Kenneth MatibaMinister under Moi until 1988. FORD-Asili leader. 1992 presidential runner-up. Died 2018.
  • Bildad KaggiaOne of the Kapenguria Six. MP and Assistant Minister 1963 to 1964. KPU Vice-President. Died 2005.
  • J.M. KariukiNyandarua North MP and Assistant Minister. Former Mau Mau detainee. Murdered 1975.
  • Pio Gama PintoJournalist and KANU MP. Assassinated 1965.
  • Wangari MaathaiFounder of the Green Belt Movement. Tetu MP and Assistant Environment Minister 2003 to 2005. Nobel Peace laureate. Died 2011.
