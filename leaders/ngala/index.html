What the record shows

  • DFormed the pre-independence government as Leader of Government Business after KANU refused to govern until Kenyatta was freed.
  • DDied after a car accident in December 1972.

What they said, and what they did

  • Said they wouldMajimbo: power shared with the regions.
    What they did
    DDissolved KADU into KANU in November 1964 as the regional system was being dismantled.

Where the evidence points, by dimension

Institutions and power-sharing: Checks and devolution (high confidence)
Led KADU's push for majimbo, the regional system written into the 1963 independence constitution to protect smaller communities.
Political style: Mixed (medium confidence)
Led the opposition at independence, then dissolved KADU into KANU in 1964 and took a cabinet post.

Contradictions in the record

  • IChampioned regional power, then joined the party that took it apart.

What they appear to have stood for

Coastal federalist who moved from opposition into government once the federal cause was lost. KADU's economic platform is not counted because he held executive power, so there are too few dimensions to match.

How to read this page

Actions come first: laws, votes, decisions, court cases. Words count less, and only where the record is thin and not contradicted. A leader with real power now is judged on actions only.
