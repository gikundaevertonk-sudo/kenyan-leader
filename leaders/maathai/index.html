Wangari Maathai

Founder of the Green Belt Movement. Tetu MP and Assistant Environment Minister 2003 to 2005. Nobel Peace laureate. Died 2011.

Never held executive power. Actions first; stated positions and reports count less.

Where the evidence points, by dimension

Social values: Leans socially progressive (medium confidence)
Built the Green Belt Movement around rural women, paying them to plant trees and organising them politically.
Land, wealth and taxes: Leans strong redistribution (medium confidence)
Fought the grabbing of public land: Uhuru Park in 1989 and Karura Forest in 1998 to 1999.
Institutions and power-sharing: Leans checks and devolution (medium confidence)
Campaigned for multiparty democracy, then served as an MP and assistant minister.
Political style: Anti-establishment (high confidence)
Confronted the Moi government for more than a decade from civil society.
Civil liberties: Liberty-first (high confidence)
Joined the 1992 hunger strike with mothers of political prisoners at Freedom Corner, where police beat her.

What they appear to have stood for

A liberty-first environmental and democratic activist who treated public land as a public trust.
