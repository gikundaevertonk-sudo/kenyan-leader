Raila Odinga

What the record shows

  • DWon a Supreme Court petition that nullified the August 2017 presidential result.
  • DRan for president five times: 1997, 2007, 2013, 2017 and 2022.
  • DLost the African Union Commission chair election in February 2025. ODM signed a cooperation agreement with Ruto's UDA in March 2025.
  • DDied on 15 October 2025.

  • Said they wouldReform and an end to the one-party order.

  • Said they wouldStood as the opposition alternative to KANU.

  • Said they wouldCalled the 2017 result stolen and was sworn in as 'people's president' in January 2018.

Where the evidence points, by dimension

Role of the state in the economy: Leans state-led (low confidence)
As Prime Minister backed state programmes such as the Kazi Kwa Vijana youth-jobs scheme. His ministerial spells left a thin economic record.
Institutions and power-sharing: Leans checks and devolution (medium confidence)
Detained for years under Moi during the fight for multiparty politics, led the 2005 'No' campaign against a strong-presidency draft and backed the 2010 Constitution. Then co-led BBI, which courts ruled unconstitutional.
Political style: Mixed (high confidence)
Alternated between confronting and joining government: merged his party into KANU in 2002, joined the Grand Coalition in 2008, the 2018 handshake, and cooperation with Ruto's government from 2025.
Civil liberties: Leans liberty-first (medium confidence)
Detained without trial 1982 to 1988, 1988 to 1989 and 1990 to 1991, and kept organising. Protests he called were met with police force that rights groups documented.

Contradictions in the record

  • IThe face of opposition who repeatedly joined or partnered with governments he opposed.
  • IChampion of the 2010 Constitution who co-led BBI, which the courts found unconstitutional.

What they appear to have stood for

A reformer on institutions and rights whose strategy moved between confrontation and deal-making.

How to read this page

Actions come first: laws, votes, decisions, court cases. Words count less, and only where the record is thin and not contradicted. A leader with real power now is judged on actions only.
