Where the evidence points, by dimension

Institutions and power-sharing: Leans checks and devolution (medium confidence)
Resigned as Justice Minister in April 2009, citing judicial appointments made without her. During the 2007 to 2008 crisis she was a leading defender of the disputed PNU result (attributed). Mixed, but leans towards checks.
Civil liberties: Leans liberty-first (medium confidence)
Has acted as defence lawyer for opposition figures in the region, including Kizza Besigye in Uganda. Tanzania deported her in May 2025 when she arrived to represent Tundu Lissu.
Political style: Leans anti-establishment (low confidence)
Left government and now leads a small opposition party.
Land, wealth and taxes: Leans strong redistribution (medium confidence, stated position)
S Her June 2026 PLP alternative budget rejects taxes on mobile money, internet services and digital platforms and would cap Social Health Authority contributions at Sh500 per family. She never ran economic policy, so no record contradicts it.
Role of the state in the economy: Mixed (low confidence, stated position)
S The same plan pairs a spending freeze and 60% cuts to discretionary spending with Sh285 billion for fertiliser subsidies, irrigation and grain reserves. It points both ways.
Social values: Mixed (low confidence, stated position)
S A long-standing advocate for women in leadership and Kenya's first woman major-party running mate (2022). In 2023 she joined Azimio leaders who condemned the Supreme Court's LGBTQ registration ruling; no direct quote from her was found. Mixed.

Contradictions in the record

  • IServed in a government whose record includes the Anglo Leasing scandals. Her own role was not examined in this pass.
  • IChampions limits on the executive from outside government. Her record inside it deserves the same scrutiny.

What they appear to have stood for

On what she did in and after office: a constitutionalist with a rights-first bent. Her economic positions come from her 2026 alternative budget, which counts as a stated position and weighs less than her record.
