Who they are

Kithure Kindiki: Deputy President since 1 November 2024. Interior CS 2022 to 2024. Tharaka-Nithi Senator 2013 to 2022. UDA. On his record in office: an order-first security minister who became the President's deputy. His economic record is the government's, not his own.

Peter Salasya: Mumias East MP since 2022. 2027 presidential aspirant. Mostly words so far: a populist, anti-establishment voice who voted against the 2024 Finance Bill and defends constituency funds. Little legislative record, so his placements are light.
