Who they are

Rigathi Gachagua: Deputy President 2022 to Oct 2024 (impeached). Former Mathira MP. Leader of DCP. On his record as Deputy President he championed the government's tax measures, so his new tax-cutting promises are not counted. His socially conservative statements are counted as stated positions. His faith-based and regional messaging is not counted, because he has held executive power. The clearest documented pattern is a shift from government insider to opposition leader.

Martha Karua: Justice Minister 2005 to 2009 (and earlier Water Minister). Former Gichugu MP. Leader of PLP. On what she did in and after office: a constitutionalist with a rights-first bent. Her economic positions come from her 2026 alternative budget, which counts as a stated position and weighs less than her record.
