Who they are

Fred Matiang'i: Education CS 2015 to 2018. Interior CS 2018 to 2022. Jubilee leader. A technocratic, state-delivery style of governing with an order-first approach to security, which his campaign promises repeat. Most placements here are low confidence.

Okiya Omtatah: Busia Senator since 2022. Public-interest litigant. Leader of the National Reconstruction Alliance. Weighing a 2027 presidential run. A checks-and-balances litigant who uses the courts against the executive and resists the sale of state assets.
