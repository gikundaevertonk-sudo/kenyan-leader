On the issues voters rank highest

  • Cost of living

    Ruto: D Signed the Finance Act 2023, which doubled VAT on fuel from 8% to 16% from 1 July 2023. After a sharp fuel price rise in April 2026 he cut it back to 8% for three months.

    Sifuna: S Cut taxes on ordinary Kenyans within 100 days, through a supplementary budget, and cut State House spending (July 2026).

  • Healthcare

    Ruto: D Replaced NHIF with the Social Health Authority (SHA) in October 2024. The Treasury says over 31 million people had registered by June 2026. The Controller of Budget found SHA owed county health facilities Sh26.87 billion at 31 March 2026, more than three times the Sh8.29 billion owed three months earlier.

    Sifuna: A Disputes government claims that SHA works, saying Bungoma's governor told him county hospitals had not been paid. His account of a private conversation.

Who they are

William Ruto: President since 2022. Deputy President 2013 to 2022. Party: UDA. Pragmatic, executive-centred developmentalism with IMF-aligned fiscal consolidation, adjusting to coalition needs. Confidence is high that the record is large, and only medium that any single label fits.

Edwin Sifuna: Nairobi Senator since 2017. Removed as ODM Secretary-General. Linda Mwananchi presidential candidate. A clear anti-establishment turn within party politics, now with a tax-relief and civil-liberties platform. His policy placements rest on stated positions.
