On the issues voters rank highest

  • Cost of living

    Ruto: D Signed the Finance Act 2023, which doubled VAT on fuel from 8% to 16% from 1 July 2023. After a sharp fuel price rise in April 2026 he cut it back to 8% for three months.

    Musyoka: S Urged voters to reject the Finance Bill 2026 and promises tax reform to ease the cost of living.

  • Corruption

    Ruto: D Signed the Conflict of Interest Act on 30 July 2025, which makes public officers declare their interests and assets under EACC oversight.

    Musyoka: S His Komboa Kenya agenda promises to recover stolen public money.

  • Healthcare

    Ruto: D Replaced NHIF with the Social Health Authority (SHA) in October 2024. The Treasury says over 31 million people had registered by June 2026. The Controller of Budget found SHA owed county health facilities Sh26.87 billion at 31 March 2026, more than three times the Sh8.29 billion owed three months earlier.

    Musyoka: S Would scrap SHA and return to NHIF: 'I would go back to NHIF and stop the stealing' (29 March 2026).

Who they are

William Ruto: President since 2022. Deputy President 2013 to 2022. Party: UDA. Pragmatic, executive-centred developmentalism with IMF-aligned fiscal consolidation, adjusting to coalition needs. Confidence is high that the record is large, and only medium that any single label fits.

Kalonzo Musyoka: Vice President 2008 to 2013. Former minister. Wiper leader. United Opposition principal. A long-serving establishment figure now leading street-level opposition, with a pro-devolution, cost-of-living platform. Most of his placements rest on stated positions, which weigh less than actions. His ministerial decisions are the next thing to compile.
