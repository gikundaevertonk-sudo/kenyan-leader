Who they are

Martha Karua: Justice Minister 2005 to 2009 (and earlier Water Minister). Former Gichugu MP. Leader of PLP. On what she did in and after office: a constitutionalist with a rights-first bent. Her economic positions come from her 2026 alternative budget, which counts as a stated position and weighs less than her record.

David Maraga: Chief Justice 2016 to 2021. United Green Movement (UGM) presidential candidate for 2027. A constitutionalist and rights-first reformer whose record is strongest on institutions and civil liberties. His economic positions are stated plans, not a record.
