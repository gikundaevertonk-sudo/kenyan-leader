Who they are

Jomo Kenyatta: Prime Minister 1963. President 1964 to 1978. KANU. Died 1978. State-centred, conservative nation-building: a strong presidency, a market economy and little room for organised dissent.

Jaramogi Oginga Odinga: Vice President 1964 to 1966. Founded KPU and later co-founded FORD. Died 1994. Left-leaning nationalist and opposition pioneer. His economic programme was never tested in office, so it is not counted.
