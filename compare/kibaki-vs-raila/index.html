Mwai Kibaki vs Raila Odinga

They differ most on role of the state in the economy, political style, civil liberties. They sit close on institutions and power-sharing.

Who they are

Mwai Kibaki: Finance Minister 1969 to 1982. Vice President 1978 to 1988. President 2002 to 2013. Died 2022. A technocratic, growth-first, cautious reformer. Institutional change came mostly under pressure.

Raila Odinga: Energy Minister 2001 to 2002. Roads Minister 2003 to 2005. Prime Minister 2008 to 2013. ODM. Died October 2025. A reformer on institutions and rights whose strategy moved between confrontation and deal-making.
