Who they are

Kalonzo Musyoka: Vice President 2008 to 2013. Former minister. Wiper leader. United Opposition principal. A long-serving establishment figure now leading street-level opposition, with a pro-devolution, cost-of-living platform. Most of his placements rest on stated positions, which weigh less than actions. His ministerial decisions are the next thing to compile.

Fred Matiang'i: Education CS 2015 to 2018. Interior CS 2018 to 2022. Jubilee leader. A technocratic, state-delivery style of governing with an order-first approach to security, which his campaign promises repeat. Most placements here are low confidence.
