Who they are

Okiya Omtatah: Busia Senator since 2022. Public-interest litigant. Leader of the National Reconstruction Alliance. Weighing a 2027 presidential run. A checks-and-balances litigant who uses the courts against the executive and resists the sale of state assets.

Peter Salasya: Mumias East MP since 2022. 2027 presidential aspirant. Mostly words so far: a populist, anti-establishment voice who voted against the 2024 Finance Bill and defends constituency funds. Little legislative record, so his placements are light.
